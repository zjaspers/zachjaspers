Customer Story Selector

A presales knowledge system for finding the right customer evidence for a specific buyer conversation, then combining multiple proof stories into one coherent narrative instead of relying on memory, logo slides, or a generic case-study list.

Launch live app ↗See how it works ↓
What it is for

Make customer proof usable in the moment.

Enterprise sellers usually have plenty of customer stories. The harder problem is retrieval: which story actually fits this buyer, this pain, this industry, and this stage of the conversation?

What breaks

Proof often lives in tribal knowledge. The best story depends on who happens to remember a customer, a metric, or a use case at the right moment.

What I wanted instead

A structured library that ranks evidence by context and helps a seller build a narrative from several relevant proof points, not just pick one logo.

How I use it

The important part

The storyboard turns proof into a narrative.

Why this matters

Different stories can prove different parts of the same business case.

A single customer example may prove adoption, another may show operational execution, and another may show labor or financial impact. The storyboard lets the seller assemble those examples side by side and create one coherent story for the buyer rather than treating each case study as an isolated artifact.

Outputs

Proof that can move with the conversation.

Output 01

Best-fit customer evidence

A ranked set of stories based on the buyer, operating problem, industry, proof strength, and relevance to the conversation.

Output 02

Combined story narrative

A reusable storyline that connects multiple proof points into a coherent argument, with supporting discovery questions and a suggested flow.

Portfolio version

Same system. Fictionalized data.

The public version preserves the interaction model and GTM workflow while using fictionalized customer scenarios. The goal is to demonstrate the system design without exposing customer-confidential information.

Design principles

What I wanted the system to preserve.

Context beats logos

The most impressive customer is not always the most useful proof for the buyer in front of you.

Proof should be composable

Several stories can work together when each proves a different part of the business case.

Tribal knowledge should compound

Good customer evidence should become easier for the whole team to find and reuse over time.
