Customer Story Selector

A system for finding the customer proof that actually fits the conversation — then combining multiple stories into one usable narrative.

Open live app ↗How it works ↓
The problem

Good proof is often trapped in people’s heads.

The challenge is rarely finding a customer logo. It is finding the story that fits this buyer, this problem and this moment.

BeforeSearch memory, decks and case studies. Use whatever example comes to mind.
AfterMatch context to proof, inspect the strongest stories, then build a coherent narrative.
The workflow

Context → proof → story.

01

Match

Start with pain, persona, industry and search context.

02

Rank

Surface the most relevant proof for the conversation.

03

Inspect

See outcomes, angle, audience and talk track.

04

Combine

Build a storyboard when one example is not enough.

The useful part

The storyboard makes proof composable.

One story may prove visibility. Another may prove adoption. Another may prove operational execution. The board lets those examples work together instead of competing for attention.

Best-fit evidenceA ranked set of relevant customer stories.
Combined narrativeA single storyline across several proof points.
Design principle

Proof only matters when it answers the question the buyer currently has.
