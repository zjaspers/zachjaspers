Selected systems

Each artifact started with an operating problem I encountered repeatedly in enterprise GTM. The goal was not simply to automate work, but to make good judgment easier to reproduce.

Operational Discovery

Fieldnote Discovery App

A structured discovery model for capturing what a customer is trying to change, how work actually flows, who owns decisions, where variance occurs, what breaks, and how value can be proven.

Problem: discovery notes become anecdotes instead of an operating model.
Output: reusable structured concepts that can inform pilots, demos, value hypotheses, and follow-up.

Demo Strategy

Demo Script Builder

A system for turning discovery and persona context into a deliberate demo plan: what to show, why it matters, how to transition, and where to branch based on buyer priorities.

Problem: demos are often assembled feature-by-feature instead of around a decision narrative.
Output: structured persona-first demo plans and scripts.

Enablement

Demo Script Standardizer

A repeatability layer for taking raw demo material and shaping it into a consistent structure without removing the judgment that makes an enterprise demonstration relevant.

Problem: strong demos depend too heavily on individual SC style and tribal knowledge.
Output: standardized scripts that can be reused, coached, adapted, and improved.

Customer Story Selector

A searchable way to match the right customer evidence to the buyer's industry, use case, persona, and business outcome instead of relying on memory or generic logo slides.

Problem: valuable proof exists, but sellers cannot reliably find the most relevant story in the moment.

Featured walkthrough: Demo Script Standardizer

This is the best tool to demonstrate rather than publish. A short walkthrough shows the operating thinking, protects internal details, and gives me a concrete artifact to use in interviews.

Recommended 75-second recording

  1. 0–10 sec: State the problem — raw demo scripts are inconsistent and hard to coach or reuse.
  2. 10–25 sec: Show a sanitized input with rough notes, features, persona, and discovery context.
  3. 25–50 sec: Run the standardization flow and show how the material is reorganized into a clear demo arc.
  4. 50–65 sec: Highlight the output: buyer outcome, talk track, proof point, transition, and optional branch.
  5. 65–75 sec: Close with the operating value — individual SC judgment becomes reusable team infrastructure.
Recording rule: use a fictional company, fictional buyer, and generic product capabilities. The portfolio should prove the method without publishing employer IP, customer information, internal prompts, or proprietary data.

Design principles

Structure before automation

The system should clarify the decision process before technology accelerates it.

Judgment stays human

Repeatability should improve quality without turning enterprise selling into a rigid checklist.

Outputs should compound

Discovery, demos, proof, and customer learning should become reusable organizational assets.
