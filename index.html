Selected systems

Each artifact started with an operating problem I encountered repeatedly in enterprise GTM. The goal was not simply to automate work, but to make good judgment easier to reproduce.

Operational Discovery

Fieldnote Discovery App

A discovery tracking and standardization system for turning rough customer notes into a structured operating model that can be reviewed, enriched, reused, and passed downstream into solution strategy.

How I use it: export the discovery template, populate it from my notes, load it back into Fieldnote, then review and fill gaps instead of treating one call as "complete" discovery.
What it tracks: outcomes, triggers, ownership, flow, decisions, variance, coordination, exceptions, proof controls, and visibility / feedback.
Why it matters: it separates facts, hypotheses, unknowns, and source notes so the team can see what is known versus what still needs validation.

The discovery-to-demo handoff

The most important part is the handoff between the tools. Fieldnote makes discovery portable. The next system can then use that structured context rather than forcing the SC to reinterpret scattered notes every time.

1. Raw discoveryCall notes, workshop observations, emails, documents, and open questions.
→
2. FieldnoteNormalize the material, reload the template, validate what is known, and intentionally expose gaps.
→
3. Demo systemUse the refined discovery output to generate and standardize a demo narrative tied to the buyer's operating reality.
Output path 01

Structured discovery record

A durable record of the customer's operating model: what they are trying to change, how work happens today, where decisions and exceptions occur, what is known, and what still needs validation.

Output path 02

Demo-ready context

A concise, reusable input for the downstream demo system so persona, business problem, workflow, proof requirements, open questions, and relevant branches can shape the story.

Design principles

Discovery is iterative

A completed call is not completed discovery. The system should make missing context visible and give the team a way to return to it.

Structure before automation

The system should clarify the operating problem before technology accelerates the production of a demo or deliverable.

Outputs should compound

Discovery, demos, proof, and customer learning should become reusable organizational assets instead of disappearing into one opportunity.
