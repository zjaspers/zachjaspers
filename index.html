A little background

I’ve spent my career where selling and solving meet.

I have more than 13 years of experience across business development, RFP strategy, implementation consulting and enterprise Solution Consulting. I joined WorkJam early and grew with the company from implementation into strategic enterprise presales.

The recurring pattern is simple: when the stakes go up and the path gets less obvious, I tend to get pulled in. I like understanding how a customer actually operates, finding the real decision underneath the request, and turning that ambiguity into something people can act on.

Over time, I started building tools around that work — not because every problem should be automated, but because good judgment should compound.

Why I build this way

Strong judgment shouldn’t disappear after one deal.

Complex enterprise selling will always require judgment. The point of these systems is not to replace it. It is to capture the useful structure around it — discovery concepts, demo architecture, customer evidence and repeatable patterns — so a team gets smarter instead of repeatedly starting over.
