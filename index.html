How I think

Good judgment should compound.

01
Understand the operating reality.

Start with how work actually happens, not the solution someone arrived asking for.

02
Turn discovery into a point of view.

A strong deal needs a thesis: the real problem, the buyer belief and the proof required next.

Standardize structure, not judgment.

Repeatable systems should make strong thinking easier to carry, not turn complex selling into a script.
