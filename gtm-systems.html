Enterprise GTM · Operating philosophy

How I build enterprise GTM systems.

The work I care about most happens between functions: where customer understanding, deal strategy, Solutions, Product and Delivery need to stay connected.

The goal is not more process. It is better judgment that survives the handoff.
The thesis

Good judgment should compound.

Complex deals generate useful intelligence. Too often it disappears into notes, individual reps or post-sale rediscovery. I want the system to carry the thinking forward.

Diagnose the seamsLook where information, ownership or expectations degrade between teams.
Standardize structureMake strong work easier to repeat without flattening judgment.
Sell for productionDesign the buying process around what can actually be delivered and adopted.

Where I start

Start where the promise meets reality.

If enterprise revenue mattered and the functions were not working together well enough, I would start with Delivery. That is where the sales promise gets tested.

Information gapContext did not survive the handoff.
Solution gapThe need was understood, but the design was weak.
Expectation gapThe customer thought they bought something different.
Product gapThe required capability genuinely does not exist.
First 90 days
