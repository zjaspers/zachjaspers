The problem

Discovery often stops at notes.

A team can capture plenty of information and still leave the real decision implicit: what is actually broken, what the customer needs to believe, and what should happen next?

DiscoveryWhat is happening in the customer’s operating model?
Deal ThesisWhat do we believe that means commercially?
The model

Evidence → judgment → action.

01

Understand

Facts, hypotheses, unknowns and the operating model.

02

Strategize

Problem, stakes, buyer belief, advantage and risk.

03

Act

Proof sequence, next move and demo context.

What changed

The customer and initiative stay the same. The level of thinking changes.

Discovery and Deal Thesis live inside the same workspace, so the strategy stays connected to the evidence that created it.

DiscoveryOutcome, trigger, ownership, flow, decisions, variance, coordination, exceptions, proof and visibility.
Deal ThesisUnderlying problem, buyer belief, structural advantage, proof sequence, risks, exclusions and next best move.
Design principle

Turn discovery into a point of view without pretending the tool can make the judgment for you.
