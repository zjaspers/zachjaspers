Operational Discovery · Project Case Study

Fieldnote Discovery App

A discovery tracking and standardization system for turning rough enterprise notes into a structured operating model that can be reviewed, enriched, reused, and passed downstream into demo strategy.

Launch live app ↗See walkthrough ↓
The problem

Discovery gets trapped in notes.

Enterprise discovery often lives as chronological call notes. Important context is present, but it is hard to see what is actually known, what is assumed, where the operating gaps are, or what still needs validation before a demo or pilot.

What breaks

Teams confuse activity with understanding. A completed discovery call can look complete even when ownership, flow, exceptions, proof, or decision rights are still unclear.

What I wanted instead

A reusable discovery model that separates facts, hypotheses, unknowns, and source material and makes the missing context visible.

How I use it

An iterative loop, not a one-time form.

01

Export

Start with the structured discovery template.

02

Populate

Add raw notes from calls, workshops, emails, or documents.

03

Reload

Load the populated file back into Fieldnote.

04

Find gaps

Review what is known, assumed, and missing.

05

Refine

Fill the gaps and carry the structured output downstream.

What it standardizes

The operating model, not the meeting order.

Operating model

Outcome, trigger, ownership, flow, decisions, variance, coordination, exceptions, proof controls, and visibility / feedback.

Evidence quality

Facts, hypotheses, unknowns, and source notes stay distinct so uncertainty is explicit rather than buried in a polished summary.

Outputs

Discovery that can travel.

Output 01

Structured discovery record

A durable view of the customer's operating reality: what they are trying to change, how work happens today, where decisions and exceptions occur, and what still needs validation.

Output 02

Demo-ready context

A reusable handoff into my downstream demo workflow so the script can reflect goals, personas, process, proof requirements, and unresolved questions.

Design principles

What I wanted the system to preserve.

Discovery is iterative

A call ending does not mean understanding is complete.

Structure exposes uncertainty

Assumptions and unknowns should be visible rather than hidden.

Outputs should travel

Discovery becomes more valuable when its structure directly informs demos, pilots, and value hypotheses.
