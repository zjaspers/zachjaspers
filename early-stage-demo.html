Early-Stage Demo Builder · Project Story

A better starting point for every first demo.

I built a tool to make my own demo prep easier: pick who I’m meeting with, start with a reusable story, and adapt it to what I’ve learned about the buyer.

Why I built it

I kept solving the same prep problem.

With a broad enterprise platform, deciding what to leave out can be harder than deciding what to show. Before early demos, I kept working through the same questions: where should I start, what matters to this buyer, and how do I keep it focused?

I already had useful patterns from past demos. I wanted a way to reuse them without piecing the story together again each time.

What it creates

An intro deck and a plan for the demo.

I turned those patterns into three starting demo arcs: Operations, Communications, and Initiative Execution. I choose the one that fits the buyer, then use two outputs to prepare:

A customizable intro deck

The tool creates a WorkJam demo intro deck for the selected arc, giving me an opening I can tailor to the buyer and what I’ve learned in discovery.

A matching demo run sheet

A step-by-step flow with talking points for a focused, 30-minute demo, with room to adapt the scenario and explore relevant detail.

Why it helps

Less rebuilding. More attention on the buyer.

The useful part is having the opening, flow, and talking points in one place. I can focus my preparation on what’s different about this conversation, with a story to come back to when questions take us deeper.

It also makes the thinking behind my demos easier to share with another SC, instead of keeping it all in my head.

Originally built for internal use at a horizontal enterprise platform. This overview is generalized for the portfolio; customer details and internal scripts are not included.
