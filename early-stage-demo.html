First-demo system · GTM workflow

A better starting point for every first demo.

A buyer-first system for choosing the right story, running a focused first demo, and adapting it without rebuilding everything from scratch.

The problem

Broad products make it easy to show too much.

The hardest part of early-demo prep is often deciding what to leave out. I wanted a repeatable starting point that kept the meeting centered on the buyer instead of the product menu.

BeforeRebuild the story each time and risk drifting into a feature tour.
AfterStart from a proven story, then adapt only where discovery gives you a reason.
The model

Buyer → play → story → branch.

01

Buyer

Who owns the outcome in this meeting?

02

Play

Choose the business problem that matters most.

03

Story

Run a reusable core narrative.

04

Branch

Go deeper only where discovery earns it.

What it gives me

A clear opening and a usable run sheet.

The system turns the selected play into practical prep, not another methodology document.

Intro deckA concise opening I can tailor to the buyer and meeting context.
Demo run sheetA focused sequence of moments, talk tracks and optional discovery branches.
Design principle

Start with the business problem the buyer owns. Let discovery decide where the story changes.
