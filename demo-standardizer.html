Demo architecture · GTM system

Demo Script Standardizer

A system for turning discovery into a focused, structured, rehearsable enterprise demo — without turning the story into a template.

Watch walkthrough ↓
The problem

Every demo should feel specific. Prep should not start from zero.

Enterprise demos tend to become feature tours or one-off productions. I wanted a third model: reuse strong structure, then let discovery shape the story.

StandardizeThe design method, quality bar, reusable scenarios and presenter artifacts.
Keep humanThe customer problem, buyer belief, sequencing, emphasis and what gets left out.
The six questions

Decide what needs to be true before deciding what to show.

01Who am I talking to?
02What do they need to believe?
03What story makes that belief logical?
04What evidence proves it?
05Where should I go deeper?
06When have I proven enough?
What comes out

A script you can use. A rehearsal you can actually run.

The system takes the same strategy and produces two levels of detail for different moments in the prep process.

Master scriptThe complete architecture: setup, show, proof, value, transition and optional depth.
Fast rehearsalThe stripped-down version an SC can scan before the meeting and deliver naturally.
Design principle

The customer’s work is the story. The product is the evidence.
