Demo Script Standardizer

A repeatable operating system for moving from customer discovery to a relevant, structured, rehearsable enterprise demo—without standardizing away the customer story.

Problem: bespoke demos do not scaleApproach: standardize the method, not the storyRole: system design + methodology

Why I built it

The product can be consistent. The customer story cannot.

Enterprise demos usually break in one of two directions. They become feature tours organized around the product, or every Solution Consultant rebuilds the story from scratch for every opportunity.

The Standardizer creates a third model: reuse proven operating stories, demo structures and presenter artifacts, then adapt them based on discovery.

Standardize how strong demos are designed without standardizing away the customer story.

The governing idea is simple: the customer’s work is the story. The product is the evidence.

The six questions

Before deciding what to show, decide what needs to be true.

The system reduces demo architecture to six questions. They separate product capability from demo relevance and force the story to earn its way into the meeting.

01Who am I talking to?
02What do they need to believe?
03What story makes those beliefs logical?
04What evidence proves the story?
05Where should I go deeper?
06When have I proven enough?
How it works

The architect stays in control.

The system is intentionally an augmentation layer, not an autonomous demo generator. The SC still owns customer understanding, belief design, story selection, product evidence, sequencing and what gets removed.

The system acts as a thought partner, editor, challenger, script standardizer and quality reviewer.

Capability creates possibility. Relevance earns demo time.
End-to-end flow

From discovery to something an SC can actually rehearse.

Discover→Identify buyer→Define beliefs→Select story→Architect flow→Select evidence→Simplify→Standardize→Rehearse→Review→Deliver→Learn & reuse

Master script

The complete presenter artifact: scenario, persona, customer connection, operational setup, workflow, proof, value translation, transition, discovery pause and optional content.

Fast rehearsal

A stripped-down delivery mode with only the cues needed to rehearse: context, action, what to show, primary message, proof, transition and optional branch.

Reusable intelligence

Not more scripts. Better demo infrastructure.

The long-term value is the library underneath the methodology. Each strong demo can contribute a reusable scenario, transition, proof point, branch, demo state or presenter cue.

Reusable demo unitsTrigger → Persona → Decision → Action → Proof → Outcome.
Buyer-specific entry pointsThe demo begins where the buyer naturally enters the execution story.
Discovery-driven branchesThe core stays reusable while depth changes based on the customer.
Quality reviewArchitecture, customer relevance, proof and delivery are reviewed before polish.
The design principle
Not to standardize demos. To standardize the way strong demos are designed, built and delivered.

The end state is that different sellers can use the same operating system and produce demos that feel consistent in quality without feeling like the same demo.
